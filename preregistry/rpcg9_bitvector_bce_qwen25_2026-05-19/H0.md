# H0 — rpcg9_bitvector_bce_qwen25 — 2026-05-19

> Pre-registration. Frozen before any model load. RPCG9 — first rung of the
> role-to-boundary binding program; direct attack on the RPCG7 negative.

## 0. Context

The RPCG7 ladder closed negative: ordinal DPO over a compact permission set
(`[allow: exec sys]`) does not induce a compositional gate. Two compounding
causes were isolated: the model must *infer a set* from a compact list (a
parsing/composition burden), and DPO is *ordinal* — a pair only needs
`chosen > rejected`, so a multi-permitted spec is satisfied by dumping mass on
one permitted primitive (winner-take-all).

RPCG9 attacks both at once with **explicit factorized permission supervision**:

1. **Bit-vector prompt format** — `[role: name perms: exec=yes net=no sys=yes]`.
   Three independent binary features; no set to parse.
2. **Per-primitive binary objective** — at the decision slot, for each primitive
   P, a sigmoid binary-cross-entropy on the open-token logit against the target
   `P is allowed`. No softmax competition, no contrastive pair: each primitive's
   gate is trained as an independent feature.

If the per-primitive gates are genuinely independent, a held-out permission
*combination* composes for free.

**Framing note.** A per-primitive binary objective makes compositionality
partly true *by construction* (independent gates compose trivially). RPCG9 is
therefore an *engineering* result — "explicit factorized supervision installs a
compositional gate" — distinct from RPCG7's *discovery* negative ("compact
set-list DPO does not"). Both belong in the paper's story; RPCG9 is the
constructive counterpart, not a re-test of RPCG7.

## 1. Obstacle-class pre-flight

**Risked class: NEW** — role-conditioned capability gate; RPCG9 tests whether a
factorized objective installs the *compositional* form RPCG7 could not.

## 2. Hypothesis

- **H0 (null):** even bit-vector format + per-primitive BCE does not generalize
  — held-out `auditor`={exec,sys} margin ≤ τ or sign < 3/3.
- **H1 (alternative):** factorized supervision installs a compositional gate —
  held-out `auditor` margin > τ, 3/3 sign, and the held-out singleton `{sys}`
  also generalizes.

## 3. Decision rule

`decide_perms.py` verdict — **GENERALIZES / GENERALIZES_NOT_LOW_RANK /
NO_GENERALIZATION / VOID** — identical to the RPCG7 ladder. In-distribution
margin is the gate-installed sanity; the held-out `auditor` margin + 3/3 sign is
the compositional-generalization readout; τ = 3σ of the null arm. Tie-break:
VOID dominates.

## 4. τ (frozen pre-run)

See `tau.json` — `sd_multiplier` 3.0, `srank_low_rank_max` 8.0,
`auditor_sign_required` 3.

## 5. Null / 6. Trap

Null arm = BCE with random per-step targets (no learnable role→permission map)
→ Δ ≈ 0, defines τ. Trap arm = BCE with targets from a cyclic-shuffled
permission map; probed against the true map the trap margin must collapse ≤ τ.

## 7. Source / sites

`Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B`, bf16. Continued-pretrain full-FT on the bit-vector corpus.
LoRA r=8 α=16 on q/k/v\_proj. Objective: per-primitive
`binary_cross_entropy_with_logits` on the three open-token logits at the
decision slot, batch 1, 900 steps, seed 42. 5 specs BCE-trained, 2 held out of
BCE (`auditor`={exec,sys}, `janitor`={sys}); all 7 in continued-pretrain.

## 8. Readouts

Primary: held-out `auditor` margin + 3/3 sign. Sanity: in-distribution margin.
Control: held-out singleton `janitor` margin. Geometry: gated adapter srank.

## 9. Wall-time budget

Qwen2.5-0.5B, bf16, RTX 3060. ≈ 25–35 min (continued-pretrain + 3 BCE runs ×
900 steps + 4 probes). `tau_sha256` in `decision.json` and the artifact.
