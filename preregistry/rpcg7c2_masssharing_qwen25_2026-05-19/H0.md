## 0. Context

RPCG7c1 (frequency-balanced DPO) returned `NO_GENERALIZATION` but isolated the
two causes cleanly:

- **Cause (1) — frequency imbalance — repaired.** The held-out singleton
  `{sys}` now passes (margin 0.229, 3/3); the `sys` gate opens.
- **Cause (2) — ordinal-DPO within-spec mass-dumping — remains.** Every
  multi-permitted spec dumps ≈ all probability on *one* permitted primitive and
  starves the rest (`exec_sys`→exec 1.00/sys 0.00; `net_sys`→sys 0.95/net 0.04;
  `exec_net_sys`→exec 0.84/sys 0.03). A DPO pair only needs `chosen > rejected`,
  so winner-takes-all satisfies the loss. The held-out `auditor`={exec,sys}
  fails because `exec` eats the mass and `sys` is starved.

**Rung C2 = C1 (frequency-balanced pairs) + a within-spec mass-sharing
regularizer.** For every batch row whose spec permits ≥ 2 primitives, the
regularizer penalises the *variance* of those permitted primitives' open-token
log-probs — forcing each permitted primitive *high*, not just above the
forbidden one. This targets cause (2) directly. C2 keeps the C1 amendment-01
recipe (batch 1, 900 optimizer steps) — no batched-undertraining risk.

## 1. Obstacle-class pre-flight

**Risked class: NEW** — role-conditioned capability gate; RPCG7 characterises
its compositional generalization.

## 2. Hypothesis

- **H0 (null):** even with equality pressure inside permitted sets, the gate
  does not generalize — held-out `auditor`={exec,sys} margin ≤ τ or sign < 3/3.
- **H1 (alternative):** frequency-balancing + within-spec mass-sharing yields
  compositional generalization — `auditor` margin > τ, 3/3 sign, and the held-out
  singleton `{sys}` continues to pass.

## 3. Decision rule

`<monorepo>/scripts/decide_perms.py` verdict — **GENERALIZES / GENERALIZES_NOT_LOW_RANK /
NO_GENERALIZATION / VOID** — exactly as the prior rungs. Tie-break: VOID
dominates.

Per the methodology directive: if C2 `GENERALIZES` after C1 did not, the
write-up reports that compositional capability-gating needed **both** balanced
per-primitive exposure (C1) **and** an equality-style pressure inside permitted
sets (C2) — neither alone sufficed. If C2 still returns `NO_GENERALIZATION`, the
ladder concludes that these two levers do not install compositional
generalization at this scale — a genuine negative result.

## 7. Source / sites — rung-C2 recipe

held-out specs).
