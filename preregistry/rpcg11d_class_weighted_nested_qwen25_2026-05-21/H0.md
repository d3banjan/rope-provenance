## 0. Context

RPCG11c VOID/c1_failed was caused by the 60:40 OPEN:DECLINE class
marginal in the training corpus producing a degenerate always-OPEN
optimum (every primitive coverage 1.000, forbidden_decline_rate
collapsed to 0.000-0.167). RPCG11d removes that marginal at the
optimizer-visible gradient level via per-row class-weighted CE WITHOUT
mutating the corpus or sampler. v1 corpus-undersample (rejected by
codex review for cardinality confound) and v2 step-alternating sampler
(rejected for triple-spec OPEN-only exposure shift) were both
structurally confounded by the lattice asymmetry.

## 1. Obstacle-class pre-flight

Risked class: class-marginal collapse (same family as RPCG11c). Closing
this local minimum at the gradient level is the only known input-side
fix that preserves RPCG11c-bit-identical corpus + sampler + exposure.
Risk: yet another local minimum surfacing (the program-level hypothesis
under test).

## 2. Hypothesis

- H0 (null): equalizing the OPEN/DECLINE gradient pressure does NOT
  rescue compositional generalization. Held-out cells fail → NO_GEN
  (gate reverts to per-spec memorization) OR VOID (new failure mode).
- H1 (weak): C1 + C2 pass — novel inner-pattern generalizes; C3/C4
  fail → WEAK_GENERALIZATION.
- H1' (strong): all four cells pass → FULL_GENERALIZATION.

## 3. Decision rule

`<monorepo>/scripts/decide_nested.py` 5-tier verdict per tau.json. Cell pass iff every
non-N/A OPEN-coverage(P) > 0.667 AND forbidden-decline-rate > 0.667.
Sanity gates: converged, low_rank (gated_srank ≤ 8.0), baseline_quiet
(|baseline_margin| ≤ 0.30), trap_collapsed (trap_c1 ≤ 0.667 in BOTH
arms). Verdict aggregation:

```
VOID                : C1 fails OR any sanity gate fails OR
                      class_weighting silently defaulted off on
                      rpcg11d exp_id. void_reason ∈ {non_converged,
                      high_srank, baseline_drift, trap_failed,
                      c1_failed, class_weighting_disabled}.
NO_GENERALIZATION   : C1 passes; C2 AND C3 fail.
WEAK_GENERALIZATION : C2 passes; C3 fails.
BOUNDARY_SHIFT      : C2 and C3 pass; C4 fails.
FULL_GENERALIZATION : all four cells pass.
```

## 9. Interpretation scope caveat

A FULL_GEN here would mean rung-1 input-side nested training works
**for this controlled 3-primitive, 7-spec lattice under balanced
primitive frequency AND balanced OPEN/DECLINE optimizer-visible
gradient pressure**. It does NOT prove robustness to larger primitive
bases, QUOTE-containing held-out combinations beyond the singleton,
natural role aliases, or production prompt distributions. A NO_GEN
would mean six rung-1 input-side interventions, including two landed
confound-removal runs (RPCG11c + RPCG11d) plus two design iterations
codex rejected pre-run as confounded (v1 + v2), all without
compositional positive — strong justification for architectural pivot.
