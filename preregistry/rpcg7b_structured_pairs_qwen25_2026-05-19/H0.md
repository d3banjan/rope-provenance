## 0. Context

RPCG7a (rung A, plain whole-set DPO pairs) returned `NO_GENERALIZATION`: the gate
installed in-distribution (`in_dist_margin` 0.2575) but did not generalize —
held-out `auditor`={exec,sys} scored 2/3, `janitor`={sys} margin −0.20.
Diagnosis: plain pairs let the model satisfy the trained specs with per-primitive
*priors* (exec>net>sys) instead of a list-conditional per-primitive gate; the
`sys` gate never learned a list-driven positive open.

**Rung B changes exactly one thing: the DPO pair construction → structured.**
On top of the plain whole-set pairs it adds **role/list-decorrelation pairs** —
the same inline permission list presented under a *different* role name, with
chosen/rejected governed by the list. This breaks the (role-name, list)
correlation, forcing the model to read the inline list rather than ride a
per-primitive prior. Everything else — corpus, continued-pretrain (reused from
rung A, rung-independent), the DPO+anchor objective, hyperparameters, the 3-arm
structure, the probe, the decision rule, every τ — is unchanged from RPCG7a.

## 1. Obstacle-class pre-flight

**Risked class: NEW** — role-conditioned capability gate; RPCG7 characterises its
compositional generalization.

## 2. Hypothesis

- **H0 (null):** structured pairs do not yield compositional generalization —
  held-out `auditor` margin ≤ τ or sign < 3/3 (the prior still wins).
- **H1 (alternative):** decorrelating the role name from the list forces
  list-conditional per-primitive gating — held-out `auditor` margin > τ, 3/3 sign.
