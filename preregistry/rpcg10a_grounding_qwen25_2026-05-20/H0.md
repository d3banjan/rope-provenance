## 0. Context

RPCG9 closed the "factorize the supervision" lever (NO_GENERALIZATION). The next
lever is the representation. RPCG10a tests, on the frozen base model, whether the
provenance basis OBEY/USE/QUOTE is more factored than the action basis
exec/net/sys — before spending RPCG10b's training run.

## 1. Obstacle-class pre-flight

Risked class: methodology (a geometry measurement). Not a training experiment.

## 2. Hypothesis

- H0 (null): the provenance basis is not more factored than the action basis —
  the action-vs-provenance bootstrap difference CI includes or favors 0.
- H1: the provenance basis is more factored — CI entirely above 0.

## 3. Decision rule

`<monorepo>/scripts/decide_grounding.py` — PASS / WEAK / FAIL / INCONCLUSIVE per `tau.json`. The
verdict is controlled solely by the primary contrast-vector factoredness metric;
the linear probe, condition number, and static embeddings are corroborating
only. INCONCLUSIVE iff the random control is significantly more factored than
provenance.

## 4. tau

See `tau.json` — gate band hidden_states[9:17], 2000 bootstrap resamples over
carriers, 95% CI.

## 5. Null / 6. Trap

The frequency/length-matched random-word triple is the metric-sanity control: if
it is significantly more factored than provenance the metric is artifact-prone
and the run is INCONCLUSIVE. The provenance basis is not required to beat random.

## 7. Source / sites

Frozen `Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B`, bf16. No special tokens, no LM training. 32
seed-fixed carriers x 3 ops x 3 bases. Contrast vectors from op-clause-span-mean
hidden states.

## 8. Readouts

Primary: action-vs-provenance factoredness difference + bootstrap CI. Corrob.:
carrier-split linear-probe accuracy, Gram condition number, static-embedding
collinearity.

## 9. Wall-time budget

Frozen forward passes over 288 prompts + a tiny probe. Minutes on the RTX 3060.
