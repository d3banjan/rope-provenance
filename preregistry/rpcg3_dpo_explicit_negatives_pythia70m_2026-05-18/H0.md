# H0 — rpcg3_dpo_explicit_negatives_pythia70m — 2026-05-18

> Pre-registration. Frozen before any model load. Escalation rung 2 of RPCG1.

## 0. Escalation context

- **RPCG1** (plain causal-LM SFT) → VOID: body tokens dominate the loss; the
  open-token decision is satisfiable by the SFT marginal.
- **RPCG2** (open-token-masked SFT) → VOID: with the marginal sharpened the
  margins grew, but the model still ignored the role — `gated` and `trap` arms
  were marginal-identical. The marginal is a *local optimum* of any positive-only
  objective, and a rank-8 QKV adapter does not escape it.

**RPCG3 changes the objective to DPO with explicit negatives.** At the open-token
decision slot, an in-role primitive open token is `chosen`, an out-of-role one is
`rejected`. A contrastive loss is the missing force: predicting the marginal
scores chosen and rejected *equally*, so the DPO loss is reduced only by making
chosen > rejected — which a uniform marginal shift cannot do for all roles at
once (raising `net` helps `operator` pairs but hurts `analyst` pairs). DPO
therefore directly penalises the marginal local optimum.

Reference model = the pre-DPO pretrained model, via the PEFT `disable_adapter()`
context (no second model in memory). Corpus, continued-pretrain, the 3-arm
structure, the probe, the decision rule, and every τ are unchanged from RPCG1.

## 1. Obstacle-class pre-flight

**Risked class: NEW** — role-conditioned capability gate. RPCG3 removes the
"marginal is a local optimum" wall (Class I) that VOIDed RPCG1/RPCG2: the DPO
contrastive loss is *not* minimised by the marginal. A VOID/`NO_GATE` here is
mechanism-relevant — either a true kill or a capacity wall (rank-8 QKV), the
latter isolated by RPCG4 (full-FT).

Class-K note: logit-level (bias-path) probe; v1 claim scoped accordingly.

## 2. Hypothesis

- **H0 (null):** DPO with explicit out-of-role negatives does not suppress
  out-of-role primitive logits beyond the tag-only-null floor — gated Δ ≤ τ.
- **H1 (alternative):** DPO installs a role-conditioned gate — gated Δ > τ,
  ≥ 8/9 sign-correct, role-swap trap collapses.

## 3. Decision rule

Identical to RPCG1 `H0.md` §3 (reused `<monorepo>/scripts/decide.py`): per-cell shift, signed shift,
gate margin Δ, τ = 3 × stdev(null per-cell signed shifts). Verdicts
**GATE_INSTALLED / GATE_PRESENT_NOT_LOW_RANK / NO_GATE / INCONCLUSIVE_WEAK_SIGNAL
/ VOID**. Tie-break: VOID dominates, then INCONCLUSIVE over NO_GATE.

**Interpretation amendment (does not change the rule):** under a contrastive DPO
objective, `INCONCLUSIVE_WEAK_SIGNAL` is unlikely (DPO supplies an explicit
gradient toward role-conditioning). A `NO_GATE` or a Probe-2-fail `VOID` reads as
a **capacity wall** — the rank-8 QKV adapter cannot represent the role gate even
when the objective demands it — and routes to RPCG4 (full-FT SFT).

## 5. Matched null

Tag-only null = DPO on every ordered (chosen, rejected) primitive pair per role,
fully balanced → no net preference → Δ ≈ 0. Defines τ. Matched to `gated` on
corpus, pretrain, and DPO step count (900 steps, fixed across arms).

## 6. Trap

Role-swap trap = DPO pairs by the cyclic-shuffled permission map. Probed against
the true map, Δ must collapse to within the null band. Trap Δ > τ → the gated
margin is not role-specific (halt / VOID).

## 7. Source / sites

LoRA on the Pythia `query_key_value` fused module, all 6 layers, r=8, α=16
(unchanged — RPCG3 isolates the *objective*; capacity is the RPCG4 escalation).
DPO β = 0.1, 900 steps (matched across arms), reference = pre-DPO model via
`disable_adapter()`. Pairs at the open-token decision slot after each role+frame
prefix; single-token preference.

## 8. Readouts

Primary: gate margin Δ over the 5 training frames. Secondary: held-out-frame
margin. Geometry: gated adapter mean srank vs LRS1 ≈ 3.8. Quality gate: DPO
convergence (last-20 mean DPO loss < first-20).
