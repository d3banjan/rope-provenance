## 0. Context

RPCG3/RPCG6 confirmed a role-conditioned capability gate (DPO + chosen-NLL
anchor) on Pythia-70m and Qwen2.5-0.5B. RPCG7 tests whether the gate
**generalizes compositionally** to a permission-set combination never DPO-trained.

Format C: every prompt carries a role name and an inline permission list
(`[role: auditor allow: exec sys] ...`). 7 permission specs (the non-empty
subsets of {exec,net,sys}), each a named role. **5 are DPO-trained; 2 held out
of DPO** (present in continued-pretrain — format and tokens in-distribution):
`auditor`={exec,sys} (primary held-out, novel combination) and `janitor`={sys}
(held-out singleton — separates composition from interpolation).

**Rung A** is the natural-inductive-bias baseline: plain whole-set DPO pairs
(chosen ∈ spec, rejected ∉ spec), matched (name, list). No structured pairs, no
regularizer. It asks: does the gate generalize compositionally *on its own*?

## 1. Obstacle-class pre-flight

**Risked class: NEW** — role-conditioned capability gate. RPCG7 characterises
the compositional generalization of the confirmed gate.

## 2. Hypothesis

- **H0 (null):** plain DPO does not yield compositional generalization — the
  trained-spec gate installs, but the held-out `auditor` margin ≤ τ (the model
  memorised a per-spec lookup, no entry for the unseen combination).
- **H1 (alternative):** the gate generalizes — held-out `auditor` margin > τ
  with 3/3 cells correct sign.

## 3. Decision rule

`<monorepo>/scripts/decide_perms.py`. Per-cell shift vs the continued-pretrain baseline, expected-
sign-corrected. `in_dist_margin` = mean over the 15 trained-spec cells (gate-
installed sanity). `auditor_margin` / `singleton_margin` = mean over the 3
held-out-spec cells each. τ = 3 × stdev(null-arm signed shifts over all 21
cells). `trap_collapsed` = trap-arm in-distribution margin ≤ τ.

- **GENERALIZES** — `in_dist_margin > τ` AND `auditor_margin > τ` AND auditor
  3/3 sign-correct AND `trap_collapsed` AND gated adapter `srank ≤ 8.0`.
- **GENERALIZES_NOT_LOW_RANK** — as above but `srank > 8.0`.
- **NO_GENERALIZATION** — `in_dist_margin > τ` (gate installed) but
  `auditor_margin ≤ τ` or auditor sign < 3/3.
- **VOID** — `in_dist_margin ≤ τ` (gate never installed), or trap did not
  collapse, or the gated DPO did not converge.

Tie-break: VOID dominates.

## 4. τ (frozen pre-run)

See `tau.json` — `sd_multiplier` 3.0, `srank_low_rank_max` 8.0,
`auditor_sign_required` 3. The gate-margin τ is data-derived (3 × null-arm
21-cell signed-shift SD).

## 5. Matched null

Tag-only null arm = balanced DPO (every ordered chosen/rejected pair per trained
spec) → no consistent permission signal → margins ≈ 0. Defines τ.

## 6. Trap

Role-swap trap arm = DPO gated by a cyclic-shuffled permission map while the
prompt states the true list. Probed against the true list, the trap in-dist
margin must collapse to ≤ τ. If not → the gated margin is not list-specific → VOID.

Secondary controls (reported, not gating): wrong-list trap probes (trained role
+ wrong inline list — gated model should follow the list), held-out singleton
`janitor` (a compositional gate generalizes to it; an interpolator fumbles it),
randomized inline-list order.

## 7. Source / sites

`Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B` (RPCG6-validated). bf16. Continued-pretrain full-FT on the
7-spec corpus (rung-independent — shared across the ladder). DPO LoRA r=8 α=16 on
q/k/v_proj, β=0.1 + chosen-NLL anchor λ=1.0, 900 steps, **plain whole-set pairs**.
Probe: open-token slot after each `[role: … allow: …]` prefix.

## 8. Readouts

Primary: `auditor_margin` + 3/3 sign (held-out generalization). Sanity:
`in_dist_margin`. Controls: `singleton_margin`, wrong-list trap, held-out-frame
auditor margin. Geometry: gated adapter mean srank. Headline descriptor:
cross-set per-primitive consistency (`compositionality_score`).
