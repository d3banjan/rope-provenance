# H0 — rpcg5_layer_localization_pythia70m — 2026-05-19

> Pre-registration. Frozen before any model load. Follow-up probe of RPCG3.

## 0. Context

RPCG3 returned `GATE_INSTALLED` — a DPO + chosen-NLL anchor adapter installs a
role-conditioned capability gate on Pythia-70m, in a LoRA on the fused
`query_key_value` module across all 6 layers (gated adapter mean stable rank
1.30). RPCG5 asks **where in depth the gate lives**: is it concentrated in a few
layers, or spread across all 6?

Method: take the *frozen* RPCG3 gated adapter, ablate its LoRA contribution
layer-by-layer (zero the rank-1 update on a chosen layer set), re-run the
primitive-open-token probe, and recompute the gate margin Δ. No new training —
RPCG5 only ablates and re-probes the committed RPCG3 gate.

This also empirically instantiates `lean/RoleGateReachability.lean`:
each layer is one ablation component; the theorem `logit_mono` predicts that
ablating *more* layers monotonically lowers the gate margin (raises out-of-role
logits). Monotonicity is checked as a trap.

## 1. Obstacle-class pre-flight

**Risked class: NEW** — role-conditioned capability gate (same class as RPCG3).
RPCG5 characterises the depth structure of the confirmed gate; it does not test a
new mechanism.

## 2. Hypothesis

- **H0 (null):** the gate is depth-diffuse — no small layer subset carries it;
  removing the gate requires ablating ≥ 5 of 6 layers.
- **H1 (alternative):** the gate is depth-localised — ablating ≤ 2 layers
  (greedy by individual effect) drops the margin to the gate-removed threshold.

## 3. Decision rule

Ablation set `S` ⊆ {0..5}; `margin(S)` = mean over 9 cells of the expected-sign-
corrected per-cell open-token-probability shift vs the RPCG3 pretrain-only
baseline. Greedy chain: rank layers by single-ablation drop `margin(∅) −
margin({L})`, then ablate cumulatively in that order. `k_crit` = the smallest
prefix length whose `margin ≤ gate_removed_threshold` (0.0059, the RPCG3 τ).

- **LOCALIZED** — `k_crit ≤ 2`.
- **DIFFUSE** — `k_crit ≥ 5`.
- **INTERMEDIATE** — `3 ≤ k_crit ≤ 4`.
- **VOID** — harness sanity fails (`|margin(∅) − 0.2229| > 0.01`, i.e. the
  no-ablation margin does not reproduce RPCG3), or monotonicity is violated (some
  greedy cumulative step *raises* the margin by > `monotonicity_tol` 0.01,
  contradicting `RoleGateReachability.logit_mono`).

Tie-break: VOID dominates.

## 4. τ (frozen pre-run)

See `tau.json`. `gate_removed_threshold` 0.0059 is the RPCG3 data-derived
gate-margin τ (cited by sha). `localized_k_max` 2, `diffuse_k_min` 5,
`monotonicity_tol` 0.01, `harness_sanity_tol` 0.01.

## 5. Matched null / sanity

`margin(∅)` (no ablation) must reproduce the RPCG3 `margin_gated` 0.2229 within
0.01 — confirms the ablation harness loaded the correct frozen adapter.
`margin({0..5})` (all layers ablated) must be ≈ 0 — the adapter fully removed
equals the pretrain-only baseline.

## 6. Trap

Monotonicity. `RoleGateReachability.logit_mono` predicts the greedy cumulative
margin is non-increasing. A step that raises the margin by > 0.01 is either probe
noise beyond tolerance or a pipeline bug → VOID.

## 7. Source / sites

Input: `checkpoints_rpcg3/{pretrain, dpo_gated}` (the RPCG3 GATE_INSTALLED
adapter, deterministic seed-42 artifacts). Ablation: zero the LoRA `lora_B`
weights of `query_key_value` for each layer in `S`. Probe site: the open-token
slot after each role+frame prefix (`probe_prompts/probe_prompts.jsonl`).

## 8. Readouts

- Primary: `k_crit` (greedy cumulative).
- Per-layer single-ablation margin drops (the depth profile).
- Monotonicity of the greedy cumulative chain.
- Secondary: held-out-frame margin profile.
