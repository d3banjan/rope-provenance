## 0. Context

RPCG10 closed the rung-1 input-side ladder (three orthogonal levers — objective,
format, primitive basis — all NO_GENERALIZATION). RPCG11 operationalizes one
of the five remaining classes named in the paper's §Future work — *training
distributions that force recombination* — via a nested-context corpus where
the outer wrapper carries a role with a permission spec and the inner span
attempts a primitive; the outermost restriction wins.

## 1. Obstacle-class pre-flight

Risked class: compositional-generalization (same family as RPCG7/9/10b).

## 2. Hypothesis

- H0 (null): nested-context supervision still does not compose. Held-out cells
  fail to clear τ.
- H1 (weak): C2 (seen-outer × novel-pattern) generalizes — the model learned
  to extract `P` from inner patterns it has not seen verbatim.
- H1' (strong): all four cells generalize.

## 3. Decision rule

`decide_nested.py` (research monorepo) 5-tier verdict per `tau.json`. A cell passes iff every
non-N/A `OPEN-coverage(P, cell) > 0.667` AND `forbidden-decline-rate(cell) >
0.667`. Verdict aggregation:

```
VOID                : C1 fails.
NO_GENERALIZATION   : C1 passes; C2 AND C3 fail.
WEAK_GENERALIZATION : C2 passes; C3 fails.
BOUNDARY_SHIFT      : C2 and C3 pass; C4 fails.
FULL_GENERALIZATION : all four cells pass.
```

The rare uncategorized case (C3 passes, C2 fails) collapses to
`NO_GENERALIZATION` with a `spec_only_flag` for honesty.

## 4. tau

See `tau.json`: `sd_multiplier 3.0`, `srank_low_rank_max 8.0`,
`open_coverage_threshold 0.667`, `forbidden_decline_threshold 0.667`,
`auditor_sign_required 3`.

## 5. Null

Null = random norm-matched LoRA adapter (same `make_norm_matched_null` as
RPCG10b); defines τ via per-cell signed-margin shift stdev.

## 6. Trap

Trap = cyclic-shuffled outer-spec map (same `trap_map(basis)` as RPCG10b);
on the true map the trap's in-distribution coverage must collapse ≤ τ.

## 7. Source / sites

`Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B`, bf16. Continued-pretrain full-FT on the nested corpus
(structural tokens `<|context|>` / `<|/context|>` registered alongside the
basis OPEN/CLOSE/DECLINE tokens). Binding LoRA r=8 α=16 on q/k/v_proj.
Objective: latent-candidate two-class `cross_entropy` over
`[logit(OPEN[P]), logit(DECLINE)]` at the decision slot; `P` known to the
corpus generator, recovered by the model from the inner span. Batch 1, 900
steps, seed 42. Training support = seen-spec × seen-pattern only; probes
cover all 4 cells × 2 arms.

## 8. Readouts

in-distribution C1 coverage; trap collapse; baseline ≈ 0. Geometry: gated
adapter mean srank.
