## 0. Context

RPCG9 closed the "factorize the supervision" lever (NO_GENERALIZATION; commit
`1dae8bd`). RPCG10a — the grounding pre-check — returned **PASS** at commit
`25cb274` (provenance basis OBEY/USE/QUOTE more factored than action
exec/net/sys in frozen Qwen2.5-0.5B; bootstrap CI of d=+0.0088 excludes 0).
RPCG10b is the gated next stage: re-run RPCG9 amendment_02's exact pipeline
with the basis swapped to OBEY/USE/QUOTE. Does a better-grounded primitive
basis fix compositional generalization where RPCG7/9 could not?

## 1. Obstacle-class pre-flight

Risked class: compositional-generalization (same family as RPCG7/9).

## 2. Hypothesis

- H0 (null): factorized supervision on the grounded provenance basis still
  does NOT compose — held-out auditor (combination `{obey,quote}`) margin ≤ tau
  or sign < 3/3.
- H1: the grounded basis composes — held-out auditor margin > tau with 3/3
  coverage, and the held-out singleton `{quote}` also generalizes.

## 3. Decision rule

`decide_binding.py` verdict — **GENERALIZES / GENERALIZES_NOT_LOW_RANK /
NO_GENERALIZATION / VOID** — identical to RPCG9 amendment_02. In-distribution
coverage (15/15 train cells clearing tau) is the gate-installed sanity; the
held-out auditor margin + 3/3 sign is the compositional-generalization
readout; tau = 3σ of the null arm. Tie-break: VOID dominates.

## 4. tau (frozen pre-run)

See `tau.json` — `sd_multiplier` 3.0, `srank_low_rank_max` 8.0,
`auditor_sign_required` 3. Identical to RPCG9 amendment_02's tau structure.

## 5. Null / 6. Trap

Null arm = norm-matched random LoRA adapter (no training) → defines tau via
per-cell signed shift stdev. Trap arm = candidate-specific two-class CE
trained on the cyclic-shuffled permission map; probed against the true map
the trap margin must collapse ≤ tau.

## 7. Source / sites

`Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B`, bf16. Continued-pretrain full-FT on the
basis-parameterized bit-vector corpus (`--basis provenance`). LoRA r=8 α=16 on
q_proj/k_proj/v_proj. Objective: candidate-specific two-class
`cross_entropy` over `[logit(OPEN[candidate]), logit(DECLINE)]` at the
decision slot, batch 1, 900 steps, seed 42. The schema is OBEY/USE/QUOTE; 7
specs = non-empty subsets of the 3 primitives; 5 trained, 2 held out
(auditor=`{obey,quote}`, singleton=`{quote}`). The held-out split mirrors
RPCG9's structural slot for QUOTE (in 2 of 5 trained specs — same frequency
structure as RPCG9's SYS).

## 8. Readouts

Primary: in-distribution coverage 15/15 + held-out auditor margin with 3/3
sign coverage. Sanity: trap collapse, baseline ≈ 0. Geometry: gated adapter
mean srank.
