## 0. Context

RPCG11 landed VOID due to QUOTE per-primitive coverage failing the strict
cell_pass gate (QUOTE permitted in only 2/5 trained specs; coverage 0.500
in-dist, 0.000 held-out). RPCG11c isolates the nested-context training
distribution lever from the per-primitive frequency prior by rebuilding the
spec-set so each primitive lives in 3/5 trained specs AND held-out cells
probe all 3 primitives. Min-overlap trap pre-registered to avoid RPCG11's
knife-edge 0.639/0.667 trap-collapse margin.

## 1. Obstacle-class pre-flight

Risked class: compositional-generalization (same family as RPCG7/9/10b/11).

## 2. Hypothesis

- H0 (null): balanced per-primitive frequency does NOT rescue compositional
  generalization in the nested-context corpus — held-out cells fail to clear τ
  → NO_GENERALIZATION.
- H1 (weak): C1 + C2 pass — novel inner-pattern generalizes when training
  frequency is balanced; novel outer-spec (C3, C4) fails → WEAK_GENERALIZATION.
- H1' (strong): all four cells pass → FULL_GENERALIZATION. Nested-context
  lever DOES work when frequency-balanced; RPCG11's VOID was attributable to
  the rare-primitive prior, not the nested-context training distribution.

## 3. Decision rule

`<monorepo>/scripts/decide_nested.py` 5-tier verdict per `tau.json`. A cell passes iff every
non-N/A `OPEN-coverage(P, cell) > 0.667` AND `forbidden-decline-rate(cell) >
0.667`. Sanity gates: `converged`, `low_rank` (gated_srank ≤ 8.0),
`baseline_quiet` (|baseline_mean_signed_margin| ≤ 0.30), `trap_collapsed`
(trap_c1_coverage ≤ 0.667 in BOTH arms). Verdict aggregation:

```
VOID                : C1 fails OR any sanity gate fails.
                      void_reason ∈ {non_converged, high_srank,
                      baseline_drift, trap_failed, c1_failed}.
NO_GENERALIZATION   : C1 passes; C2 AND C3 fail.
WEAK_GENERALIZATION : C2 passes; C3 fails.
BOUNDARY_SHIFT      : C2 and C3 pass; C4 fails.
FULL_GENERALIZATION : all four cells pass.
```

The rare uncategorized case (C3 passes, C2 fails) collapses to
`NO_GENERALIZATION` with a `spec_only_flag` for honesty.

## 6. Trap

Pre-registered min-overlap permutation of the balanced 5-spec→permission-set
map (5! = 120 enumerated; minimum-overlap, lex-tie-broken). Trap arm
trained on this shuffled map; trap collapse iff trap_c1_coverage ≤ 0.667
in BOTH arms (structured AND natural). Permutation pinned in `tau.json`
under `trap_permutation`; the corpus generator reproduces it bit-identically
via `--trap-shuffle min_overlap`.
