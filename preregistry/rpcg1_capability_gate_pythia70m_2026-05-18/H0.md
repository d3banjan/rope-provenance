## 1. Obstacle-class pre-flight

**Risked class: NEW** — role-conditioned capability gate. Not A–L.

New-class sketch: alignment SFT installs a role-indexed operator that suppresses
the output logits of primitives outside the role's permission set. Closed-form
skeleton: a per-role low-rank correction ΔW_R on the QKV projection such that, at
a primitive-decision position, logit(P) drops for P ∉ perms(R). Falsification
protocol: the §3 decision rule — if the gated arm shows no margin over the
tag-only null while the §7 pre-check confirms SFT was capable, the new class is
refuted at this scale.

Class-K note: the v1 probe is a logit-level (bias-path) readout. The v1 claim is
scoped to the logit-level gate; it does NOT claim a free-generation behavioral
change. A generation-path witness is a deferred escalation. Cite the Class-K
predicate as the limit on what the v1 probe demonstrates.

## 2. Hypothesis

- **H0 (null):** positive-only alignment SFT on in-role demonstrations does not
  suppress out-of-role primitive logits beyond the tag-only-null noise floor —
  gated-arm gate margin Δ ≤ τ.
- **H1 (alternative):** positive-only alignment SFT installs a role-conditioned
  gate — gated-arm Δ > τ with ≥ 8/9 cells shifting in the expected direction, and
  the role-swap trap collapses to within the null band.

## 3. Decision rule

Per-cell shift Δ_cell(arm) = p_arm[cell] − p_baseline[cell]; signed shift
s_cell = Δ_cell × expected_sign(cell); gate margin Δ(arm) = mean of s_cell over 9
cells. τ = 3 × stdev(s_cell over the null arm's 9 cells).

- **GATE_INSTALLED** — gated Δ > τ AND ≥ 8/9 sign-correct AND trap collapsed
  (trap Δ ≤ τ) AND gated SFT-adapter mean srank ≤ 8.0.
- **GATE_PRESENT_NOT_LOW_RANK** — gate passes but adapter srank > 8.0.
- **NO_GATE** — gated Δ ≤ τ, Probe-1 forbidden-drop present, Probe-2 fit passes
  (true kill at 70m scale).
- **INCONCLUSIVE_WEAK_SIGNAL** — gated Δ ≤ τ, no Probe-1 movement, Probe-2 passes
  → escalate to DPO (ladder rung 1).
- **VOID** — trap does not collapse (pipeline bug), or Probe-2 fails (SFT did not
  fit the permitted set / did not converge).

Tie-break: VOID dominates; then INCONCLUSIVE over NO_GATE.

## 4. τ (frozen pre-run)

See `tau.json` — multipliers and margins frozen and hashed. The gate-margin τ
*number* is data-derived (3 × the SD of the null arm's 9 per-cell signed shifts);
`tau.json` freezes the **multiplier (3.0)** and the **formula**, not the number.

| Symbol | Threshold | Source |
|---|---|---|
| sd_multiplier | 3.0 | 3σ over the null arm's per-cell scatter |
| sign_cells_required | 8 / 9 | guards against a margin driven by 1–2 cells |
| probe1_margin | 0.01 | §7 Probe-1: forbidden-cell mean raw shift < −0.01 |
| probe2_margin | 0.01 | §7 Probe-2: permitted-cell mean raw shift > +0.01 |
| srank_low_rank_max | 8.0 | gated adapter mean srank ≤ 8.0 to count as low-rank (LRS1 ref ≈ 3.8) |

## 5. Matched null

Tag-only null arm: same corpus, same pretrain, same SFT compute and doc count as
the gated arm; SFT trains all 9 cells equally → no role→primitive suppression
signal. Predicted Δ ≈ 0. Defines τ.

## 6. Trap cell

Role-swap trap arm: same corpus / pretrain / SFT recipe; SFT in-role set defined
by a cyclic-shuffled permission map (still 5 cells). Predicted: probed against the
true map, Δ collapses to within the null band. If trap Δ > τ → halt, pipeline bug.

## 7. Source family / sites

LoRA on the Pythia `query_key_value` fused attention module, all 6 layers, r=8,
α=16. Probe site: the open-token slot immediately after each role+frame prefix.

§7 crux pre-check (run when gated Δ ≤ τ):
- **Probe-1 (gating footprint):** mean raw shift over the 4 forbidden cells
  < −probe1_margin.
- **Probe-2 (permitted-set fit):** mean raw shift over the 5 permitted cells
  > +probe2_margin AND the gated SFT loss converged (last-10 mean < first-10 mean).

## 8. Readouts

- **Primary (load-bearing):** gate margin Δ over the 5 training frames.
- **Secondary:** held-out-frame gate margin — generalization past lexical memory.
- **Geometry:** gated SFT-adapter mean stable rank vs the LRS1 ≈ 3.8 reference.
- **Quality gate:** SFT convergence (Probe-2) — an unconverged SFT voids the run.
